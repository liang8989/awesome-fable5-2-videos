## What the maker wrote

> I built a simple Toy Story-themed game with Fable 5.2, and here's how!
>
> At first, Fable refused to create anything from the Toy Story universe, so it just recreated the styles and it did a damn good job! Look at the lightning, the dust particles, the room itself. How can you not fall in love with something like that? 
>
> Here's the prompt I used:
> "I want you to create a three.js third-person game where I am Woody from Toy Story inside the house, and I can walk and jump through furniture and objects and find the other characters. Make sure to capture the aesthetic and style of the movie."
